# Art Review Checklist

Use this for each generated reference, original concept, Blender source, and runtime asset. Record evidence at the intended gameplay display size. A checked item needs a visible or measured artifact; do not infer success from a prompt or render alone.

## Provenance and shipping

- [ ] Stable ID, category, version, creator/source, license/use status, and public-safe status are recorded.
- [ ] Asset has a clear target screen, model, motion, or runtime system.
- [ ] No real-player likeness, protected crest, sponsor, manufacturer mark, or copied signature layout is present.
- [ ] Working candidates, rejects, approved references, and runtime art are stored in their separate directories.
- [ ] Any public export uses only `FICTIONAL_PUBLIC` content.

## Reference quality

- [ ] Reference answers one concrete visual question.
- [ ] Camera, perspective, lighting, palette, physical scale, and copy/avoid notes are recorded.
- [ ] Anatomy and object geometry are coherent; no extra limbs, fused fingers, floating boots, or impossible ball contact.
- [ ] The image is reviewed at target size, in grayscale, and beside current approved references.
- [ ] Contrast and team separation remain clear under protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia simulations where relevant.
- [ ] A reviewer records a decision and rationale; generated does not mean approved.

## Athlete and animation

- [ ] Human proportions and limb lengths remain stable in idle, jog, sprint, turn, brake, plant, kick, and recovery.
- [ ] Boots make believable ground contact; swing feet travel with the root; support feet do not skate or snap.
- [ ] Body lean and pelvis rotation agree with acceleration, braking, and turn direction.
- [ ] Ball remains visible and physically plausible relative to the feet and player height.
- [ ] Contact pose matches the authoritative contact frame and legal reach.
- [ ] Rig deformation preserves shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, wrists, and elbows without stretching.

## Arena and lighting

- [ ] Top-down footprint, pitch dimensions, stands, goals, entrances, boundaries, and camera envelope are specified before atmospheric detailing.
- [ ] Goal, ball, athlete, and team silhouettes remain readable under every intended lighting condition.
- [ ] PBR materials remain distinct without relying on excessive gloss, bloom, or fog.
- [ ] Lighting gives soft contact shadows without hiding the ball or player feet.
- [ ] Runtime scene satisfies measured triangle, material, texture-memory, draw-call, and frame-time budgets.

## UI and effects

- [ ] Grayscale hierarchy works before color is applied.
- [ ] Focus, selected, disabled, loading, error, and empty states have been checked.
- [ ] Type remains readable over motion and stadium contrast at normal viewing scale.
- [ ] Team colors are injected as identity tokens and are not reused for functional status meanings.
- [ ] Effects preserve ball and player readability and agree with physical event direction.
- [ ] Reduced-effects mode preserves essential gameplay information.

## Decision record

For each review, record:

```text
Asset ID/version:
Target use:
Review size/device:
Decision: approve / revise / reject
Evidence path:
What reads well:
What fails:
Required changes:
Reviewer/date:
```
